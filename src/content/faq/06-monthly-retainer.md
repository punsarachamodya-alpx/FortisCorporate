order: 6

A monthly retainer gives your business ongoing access to legal support — corporate, commercial, contractual, employment and compliance matters — without needing to engage us separately for every requirement. It suits businesses that need regular legal input but don't require a full-time in-house lawyer. Retainers can be capped or uncapped depending on your needs; contact us to discuss what would suit your business.
