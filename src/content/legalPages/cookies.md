This Cookie Notice explains how Fortis Corporate Services (Pvt) Ltd uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide reporting information.

## Cookies we use

This website uses **Google Analytics** to help us understand how visitors use the Site — for example, which pages are visited most, and how visitors found us. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymised or pseudonymised usage data, such as pages viewed, time spent on the Site, and general location and device information. This data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's own privacy and data policies.

We do not currently use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking purposes.

## Embedded third-party content

Some pages on this Site embed third-party content, such as Google Maps. These third-party providers may set their own cookies when their embedded content is loaded — this is governed by their respective privacy and cookie policies, not this notice.

## Managing cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting cookies. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of this and other websites. You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking using Google's [Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

We may update this Cookie Notice from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies and technologies we use. Please check back periodically.
